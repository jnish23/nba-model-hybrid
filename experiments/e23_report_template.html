3 · Do favourites on a back-to-back blow games open less often?

Observed rate of a favourite win by 15 or more, against the rate a normal margin model with the spread as its mean and σ = implies.

Every favourite cell has an observed blowout rate below the model's, by 1 to 6 points and by 1 to 3 points even for rested favourites, so the normal model overstates blowouts in general. The question is whether back-to-back favourites differ from rested ones: for spread 8+ the difference is in confirmation (interval to ) and in discovery ( to ). The mechanism points the right way and is too small to see with 137 and 219 games. The registered rule needs the confirmation interval to exclude zero, so it is not supported. Whether starters are pulled earlier cannot be tested with team totals; it would need player-minutes data (player_basic_stats exists) and was not part of the plan.

Limits

  • Cells are small (137 and 219 games for the key cell), so a real effect of one to two points would not show. The joint test over four coefficients has little power per coefficient.
  • "Back-to-back" is the training set's flag (one day of rest). Third game in four nights and travel were not tested.
  • Holm across the 8 angles is pending; the confirmation joint p of is nowhere near 0.05.
