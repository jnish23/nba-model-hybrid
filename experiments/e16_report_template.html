3 · The registered regression

As specified: regress r on the fitted overtime probability with |spread| and the opening total as controls. The registration warned that this cannot work, because the probability is a function of |spread| alone, so the coefficient is identified only through the curvature of the logistic link. The numbers confirm it.

Coefficients are in the hundreds with intervals spanning zero by hundreds, and the sign flips between periods. The "regulation-only includes zero" condition is technically met, but only because the interval is wide enough to include everything, so it carries no information. The overtime logistic itself is fine: it passes the calibration check (no confirmation decile off by more than 2 points beyond its interval).
