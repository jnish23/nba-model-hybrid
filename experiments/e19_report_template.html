Do the new anti-tanking rules matter here?

Mostly for the tanker flag, and not for the main finding. What changed (NBA release and ESPN, May 2026; sources below):

  • A 3-2-1 lottery takes effect with the 2027 Draft, so it governs the 2026-27 season's records. The lottery grows to 16 teams. The three worst records get two balls and cannot pick lower than 12th; teams from fourth-worst through tenth-worst get three balls each, which flattens the odds. Play-in seeds 9 and 10 get two balls and the losers of the 7-8 games one. A team's own pick cannot be No. 1 two years running or top five three years running, and newly traded picks cannot carry top-12 to top-15 protections. The league can cut a team's lottery odds, move its draft position and fine it (ESPN reports up to $10 million). It runs for the 2027 to 2029 drafts.
  • For resting stars the 2023 player participation policy (no more than one star unavailable per game, fines, no shutdowns) is, as far as my search shows, still the framework. I found no 2026-27 change, but that search was not exhaustive.

What it means for the test. Being the very worst no longer pays: the bottom three are penalised and records 4th through 10th worst are equal. A bottom-5 win% flag will therefore mix teams that stopped caring about losing with teams now trying to avoid the bottom three. Contenders locked into a seed keep their old reasons to rest, limited by the participation policy. No season in this sample has the 3-2-1 rules, so what E19 measures is the old regime, and whether it carries into 2026-27 can only be answered with forward data from this season. The late-season over lean below shows up in games with no tanker or locked flag, so it is less exposed to the lottery change than the tanking idea was.

Limits

  • Late means either team's game number ≥ 70. In 2020-21 (72 games) that is the last three games, and 2019-20 was cut short, so the late window is thin in two of the seven seasons. Game numbers match the training set's season_game_number columns for every game.
  • Locked means more than 6 games clear of the next seed in both directions, for conference ranks 1 to 10; I read "next seed" as both neighbours. A looser rule would flag more games.
  • Tanker is bottom 5 in win% among 30 teams at the date, from the start of the season, so most tanker games are not late.
  • The step-by-game-number table and the by-season late-minus-not-late differences are exploratory additions. The primary six-coefficient test has the least power per coefficient.
