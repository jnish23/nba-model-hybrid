# E20 pre-registration: do late-season games go OVER the opening total? (forward test)

Written 2026-10-23, after E19 and **before any E20 number exists**. Nothing is computed by this document. Criteria are not edited after the first forward game is scored; a badly specified criterion is reported as a limitation, not changed.

## Why the old data cannot confirm this

E19 found that games with either team at game 70 or later finished **+2.40 points** over the opening total on average (1,083 regular-season games, interval +1.33 to +3.46), against about +0.3 in earlier games (the difference is about +2.2).
The finding came from an exploratory look at a registered test that failed its find step, after other angles had been tried, so it carries a large multiplicity burden and an upward bias from being chosen after the fact. All 2019-20..2025-26 games are therefore treated as descriptive only in E20 and carry no weight in any criterion.
The only untouched data is the future. This is a forward test, in the same spirit as E15 (the spread slope) and on the same forward games.

## Definitions (identical to E19 so the two can be compared)

- `odds_total`, `target_total_pts`: from the training set generated by `scripts/generate_training_set.py`, same priority-book opening line as E14-E19. `r = target_total_pts - odds_total`. Push = `r == 0`.
- **Regular-season games only** (game id prefix `002`). Playoff (`004`), play-in (`005`) and NBA Cup knockout and final games (other prefixes) are excluded, because game numbers and standings exist only for the regular season.
- A team's **game number** = its regular-season games played before the game's date + 1, built from `team_game_full` results exactly as in `experiments/e19_tanking.py` (`build_flags`). **Late (L)** = either team's game number >= 70. This cutoff is fixed and is not tuned.
- Same plausibility filter as E14 (total in 170..290, |spread| <= 30).
- **Forward sample F**: 2026-27 regular-season games that are late. **Comparison sample C**: all other 2026-27 (and later) regular-season games (neither team at game 70) with a valid opening total, in the same seasons as F. Games enter as the training set is regenerated; nothing is selected by outcome.
- Intervals: day-cluster bootstrap, 1000 draws, seed 20261017, the same method as E14-E19; the difference of two group means uses paired draws.

## Hypothesis H_LATE (sign fixed now)

The mean of `r` in late games exceeds its mean in not-late games of the same seasons: `mean_r(F) - mean_r(C) > 0`.
The difference, not the late mean alone, is the primary quantity because the market total has been biased slightly upward-or-downward within a season at times (the mean of `r` over all regular-season games was +0.5 in 2019-26), and a season-wide bias should not count as a late effect.

**H_LATE is SUPPORTED iff, at the single decisive evaluation, the 5th percentile of the bootstrap distribution of `mean_r(F) - mean_r(C)` is above 0** (one-sided 5%, because the sign is fixed in advance). The two-sided 95% interval is reported beside it but does not decide.
If the bound is not above 0, H_LATE is NOT SUPPORTED, including when the point estimate is positive.

## Sample size and looks (fixed now, so there is no stopping on a lucky run)

- **Decisive evaluation: once, when F reaches 600 late games** (about three regular seasons at about 198 late games per full season; i.e. after the 2028-29 regular season, which also ends the first three drafts under the 3-2-1 lottery rules). It uses all F and C games then available.
- Non-decisional looks when F reaches 200 and 400 late games (after each of the first two seasons): the same statistics reported, no criterion changed, nothing stopped, nothing bet because of them.
- Power at the decisive size (one-sided 5%; difference-of-means standard error = sd(r) x sqrt(1/n_F + 1/n_C) with sd(r) = 18.5 and n_C about 5.1 x n_F; day clustering will make the true standard error slightly larger):

| true late-minus-not-late difference | n_F = 200 | n_F = 400 | n_F = 600 | n_F = 800 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2.2 (the E19 estimate) | 46% | 70% | 85% | 92% |
| 1.1 (half of it, allowing for the after-the-fact selection) | 19% | 29% | 38% | 46% |
| 0.7 | 12% | 17% | 21% | 25% |

  So even a real effect of the E19 size is missed about one time in seven at 600 games, and a half-size effect is missed about six times in ten. NOT SUPPORTED will mean "not shown at this sample size", not "absent".

## Regime caveat (read before interpreting)

The 3-2-1 lottery (effective with the 2027 Draft, so it governs the 2026-27 season), its pick-limit and enforcement rules, and the 2023 player-participation policy are the context for these games (sources in `reports/experiments/PREREGISTRATION.md`, section E19). **No game in the E19 sample was played under 3-2-1 rules.**
E19 showed the late over lean in late games with no tanker and no locked team as well, so it is not tied to tanking, but a change in late-season incentives could still change it. A null result here cannot separate "no effect" from "the new rules removed it"; a positive result is evidence that it persists under them.

## Secondary readings (descriptive; none changes the verdict)

1. Mean `r`, over rate and under rate (pushes dropped) in F and in C; the late minus not-late difference per season of F, to show whether one season drives a result.
2. The groups from E19: late tanker (LT), late locked (LK), late other (LO), each against C, with the E19 definitions unchanged (tanker = bottom 5 in win% at the date among 30 teams, locked = more than 6 games clear of both neighbouring ranks within ranks 1-10 of the conference). Counts are reported; LK is expected to be near empty.
3. Mean `r` by stretch of the later team's game number (1-19, 20-39, 40-59, 60-69, 70+), to see again whether the step is at game 70.
4. Open-to-close line movement (`close_total - odds_total`) for F versus C, and `actual - close_total`.
5. The deployed model's pre-game logged total prediction for the same games (the `predictions_log_v2` rows written before tip-off, never refit): its residual `actual - total_pred` in F versus C. If those logs are missing for a game it is left out; predictions are never reconstructed afterwards.

## Betting readout (reported, not a decision rule for stakes)

Flat over bet at -110 on every late game at the opening total, at the closing total, and at the best-book line and quoted price (E12 method, `experiments/totals_bets.py`), return per bet with a day-bootstrap interval.
Break-even is a 52.38% over rate. At 600 bets the interval is about +/-9 percentage points, so it can show a gross failure and cannot confirm a small edge. The under side is not bet or reported as a strategy.
No stake conclusion follows from E20 unless H_LATE is SUPPORTED **and** the best-book return point estimate is positive; even then the interval is the evidence, not the point estimate.

## If H_LATE is supported: model and feature step

Only then: (a) the model-residual comparison in secondary reading 5 must also show a late-minus-not-late residual above 0 (one-sided 5%) before any model change is proposed; (b) if it does, run the standard feature experiment (`experiments/feature_exp.py`) adding a pre-game `late_flag` (either team's game number >= 70) and `games_remaining` against the baseline and the noise floor under the common protocol in `PREREGISTRATION.md`, with the forward seasons as extra test folds. No feature is built before then, and no model is changed on E20 alone.

## Implementation

The scoring script `experiments/e20_forward_late.py` will reuse `build_flags` from `experiments/e19_tanking.py` and the paired-draw difference helper, and will be written and committed **before** the first look (200 late games). It takes the regular-season 2026-27 games as they appear and applies the definitions above without parameters.

## Choices made here that you can change before the first scoring

1. Decisive size 600 late games (three seasons). 800 (four seasons) raises power for the E19-size effect from 85% to 92% and for half of it from 38% to 46%.
2. One-sided 5% instead of the two-sided 95% used in E14, because the sign is fixed in advance. Two-sided would cut power at 600 games from 85% to 76% for a true difference of 2.2.
3. Late = game number >= 70 and the primary quantity being the difference against not-late games (not the late mean alone).
4. NBA Cup knockout and final games and play-in/playoff games are excluded.

## Out of scope

No claim about spread, moneyline or player-prop markets; no use of any 2013-14..2025-26 game as evidence; no tuning of the cutoff, the groups or the thresholds on F; no reading of the first 16 games already played in 2026-27 (they are not late and are not used).
