Totals Calibration by Level

The model under-predicts high totals. Two simple corrections fitted on the calibration block cut total error out of sample in all seven seasons. They mostly move the model toward the market line; they do not beat it.

What the fits look like

Era checks

High totals cluster in recent seasons, so a level correction can really be an era correction. Two registered variants test that: R1 refits each fix on only the later half of the calibration block, and R2 replaces the level with the opening total minus the previous 365 days' mean (past lines only). A fix is kept only if both give a lower pooled MAE.

Both fixes survive both checks with almost the same gains. Isotonic fails R1 (it gets worse).

Against the market

The baseline is worse than the market line itself on the same games ( against ), which E1 already noted. Fix (c) brings the model to , equal to the line within noise. What the correction does is stop the model from making large errors where it ignores the line at high totals; it does not give the model new information about the game. So it is worth having if you rely on the model's total, and it is not a betting edge.
