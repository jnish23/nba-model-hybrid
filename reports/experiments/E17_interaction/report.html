5 · Against the registered rules

Limits

  • Power is weak for an interaction: the formula-based detection threshold is a slope change of about across the interquartile range of the total, and the simulation in reports/experiments/POWER_CHECK/ found the registered rule catches that size about 68% of the time (false-positive rate 2.7%). A real but modest interaction is not ruled out.
  • The tercile cutoffs are the discovery ones as you specified; the confirmation distribution is skewed toward high totals, and the within-season version is an addition of mine.
