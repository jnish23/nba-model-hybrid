Is it just high totals in disguise?

No. Adding the opening total and a dummy for each season leaves the spread coefficient where it was.

Power. The plan quoted ±1.3 points for an under rate. With 1,414 games at 10+ that is one standard error (1.3 points); the 95% interval is about ±2.6, and an 80%-power test needs a shift near 3.7 points from 50%. The observed over rate of 53.4% in this bucket sits right at that limit, so a real effect of this size cannot be separated from a smaller one.

3 · Does the model already absorb it?

The deployed model's out-of-sample predictions (reports/oos_predictions_*.csv, 2021-22 to 2025-26) already lean the way the market residual points. Its prediction minus the opening total rises with spread size, and the residual that remains after the model is smaller than the market's.

4 · Overtime split

Overtime games finish about 21 points over the opening total, and they are twice as common in close games. That inflates the overs in low-spread games and makes big-spread games look relatively more under. Your suspicion about the mechanism is right, but it works against the theory: take overtime out and the slope gets steeper.

In regulation time, close games run under the line by about 1.3 points and games at 12+ run over it by about 1.4. Overtime is one reason close games look like overs, not the reason blowouts look like unders.

5 · Opening versus closing line

The market does not correct this before tip-off. Closing total minus opening total has no relationship to spread size, and the residual against the closing line has the same positive slope.

Source difference. The training-set opening total comes from one priority book. The closing total is the median across cleaned books, so the two differ by a book effect as well as by movement (mean absolute gap points where both exist). That adds noise to the open-to-close comparison but no trend with spread size.

7 · Features: not run

The registered gate needed all four of these to hold before building abs_spread or a blowout probability. None did, so no feature was built and feature_exp.py was not run.

If you want to test the opposite direction (an over lean for big spreads, which the model only half absorbs), that needs its own registration with the sign fixed in advance. I have not started it.
