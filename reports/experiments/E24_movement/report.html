Line Movement Test

Does the move from the opening line to the close carry information the close has not priced, as momentum (the move continues) or overreaction (it reverses)? First, what the odds tables actually contain.

What the odds tables are (verified before registering)

  • No timestamps. nba_odds and nba_odds_opening have no time column, only the date and a status (Final, a tip time such as "19:00 ET", or an in-game string such as "02:45 4th"). The collector's own notes say the same.
  • nba_odds is a scrape of the book prices shown on the day, described in odds_clean.py as "scraped after the game" for completed games, so it is the last pre-game line as displayed afterwards, at an unknown time just before tip. In-game prices leaked in before: two windows (2019-10-22 to 2019-11-19 and 2021-10-19 to 2021-11-23) are set to missing and books more than 3 points (spread) or 6 points (total) from the median are dropped.
  • Evidence it is mostly a closing line. The total's mean absolute error is 1.1% to 2.9% lower at the close than at the open in every season (the documented normal), and for the 16 games of 2026-27 that also have timestamped snapshots the two sources are identical to the half point.
  • nba_odds_opening is SBR's opening line, posted at an unknown time that can be a day or more before tip. So the open-to-close move is not a fixed-horizon number: its standard deviation is points for totals ( of totals and of spreads move at all).
  • odds_snapshots is now filling (hourly collector since 2026-10-19; 6,273 rows, 18 games so far; its "opening" rows mean first seen by the collector). Too few games for a test yet; the forward test below is registered for it. Several sources (SBR, The Odds API) feed it, so the book set can change between fetches.

What this means: the historical test asks whether the last displayed pre-game line has already priced everything that moved the line since an early opening. It does not say what you could do at a fixed time. If some in-game price stayed within the filters it would make the close look better than it was, which should push the slope toward overreaction, the opposite of the result below.

1 · Efficiency of the close

Regression of actual total − closing total on the total's move (close − open). Zero means the close is efficient, positive momentum, negative overreaction. Two-sided, registered primary on the first column.

3 · Relevance to the model

The model prices off the opening line and the noon information, so movement is not an input. Its residual (actual − prediction) rises with the move by points per point of move (interval to ): the move is the news the model does not have, and the close has largely priced it. That is why opening-line bets are exposed to line movement, and it is the reason the earlier reports flag the opening-line caveat.

4 · Betting at the close, with the closing prices

Bets are placed at the close after a move of at least c points, c in {2, 3, 4, 5, 6}, with the move or against it. Priced at the closing over and under quotes (the book nearest the consensus line, best price on ties); mean payout per unit on the over and on the under, about -109. The "-110" column uses the consensus line at -110.

Going with the move loses 1% to 5% per bet (at the chosen cutoff of 2 points about two points better than betting every game), and going against it loses 3% to 8%. The slope is real but about 15% of a move of 2 to 5 points is under a point of total, well inside the vig.

5 · Forward test on the timestamped snapshots (registered)

  • Source: odds_snapshots, pre-game 'current' rows, consensus across kept books. Close = the last pre-tip snapshot; T4 = the last snapshot at or before 4 hours before the scheduled start. Movement = close − T4, computed as the median of each book's own change so that a change in the book set is not read as movement.
  • Test: actual total − close on that movement, two-sided; supported iff the 95% interval excludes 0 at the single decisive evaluation at 1,000 games (about one season of collection); looks at 250 and 500 decide nothing. The same for the spread.
  • This is the version that could be acted on, since T4 is a time you can bet before. The collector's reliability (missing fetches, source switches) is reported before any result.
  • Status: 18 games collected over 4 days. The scoring script is not written yet.
