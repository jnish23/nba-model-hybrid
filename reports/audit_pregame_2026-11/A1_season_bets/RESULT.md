   (Oct 20-31) are RECOMPUTED, because `daily_inference.log` starts on Nov 1. The recomputation (matched-book snapshot price, total sigma 18.9 fitted on those same days) reproduced all 72 printed decisions per market; with the brief's flat -110 it reproduces 67 of 72 spread decisions.

   Every interval includes 0. The total bets are the one clear departure from their own claims: 11 wins where the stated edges imply 17.7 (P = 0.009), in a sample where scoring ran 2.7 points under the lines.
3. CLV (cross-book median close, 23 to 60 minutes before tip): spread +0.00 pts [-0.54, +0.47]; total +0.60 pts [+0.22, +1.25] (9 toward, 1 against, 14 unchanged); ML +1.4 pp [-0.9, +4.8]. ### F5. Edge = own view + line move (task 4; script 04 T4, script 05 P1)

Source of the opening line: the model's own input. `prediction_explanations` stores, for every explained game, a "line movement since the open" piece whose running value is the opening line the model read (`team/explain.py:181-208`: spread movement piece value = -open home spread, total movement value = open total, ML movement value = implied probability of the opening home price). Pieces are checked to add up to the model prediction (explain.py:209-211), so this is the exact input, not a proxy. It exists for 145 of 160 completed games; the 14 games of Oct 20-21 (explanations did not exist yet) use the snapshot opening proxy (4 ML, 3 spread, 6 total of the bets); game 99 has no line. The brief's proxy (`odds_snapshots` 'opening', cross-book median of each book's first opening row) versus the true input on 136 games: spread mean +0.11 pts, MAE 0.38, 68% exactly equal; total mean -0.01, MAE 0.69, 49% exact; the own-view number computed from the proxy correlates 0.70 (spread bets, n=16) and 0.94 (total bets, n=21) with the true one, and puts the bet in the same own- or move-dominated class 81% / 86% of the time. The proxy is good for lines, weak for the decomposition of a single spread bet.

Definitions (side-signed, points; ML in probability points; ML away-side open price assumes a constant overround): own = model minus the opening line the model read; move = opening minus the run-time (11 am) line, positive = the market moved AWAY from the bet side since the open (the bet fades the move); edge = own + move.

| market | bets | mean edge | mean own | mean move | fade / follow / none | own >= move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ML (pp) | 18 | 7.94 | -0.23 | 8.17 | 94% / 0% / 6% | 11% |
| Spread | 19 | 3.56 | 0.67 | 2.89 | 95% / 0% / 5% | 26% |
| Total | 29 | 5.44 | 3.99 | 1.45 | 59% / 17% / 24% | 66% |
| ALL | 66 | | | | 79% [60, 92] / 8% / 14% | 39% |

Fade-share CIs: ML 94% [79, 100], spread 95% [81, 100], total 59% [38, 80]. Results by what dominates: own-view-dominated 26 bets 9-17, -33.8% [-63.4, +0.4]; move-dominated 40 bets 20-20, -2.7% [-28.9, +24.2]; fading the move 52 bets 25-27 -6.7% [-29.9, +22.7]; following or no move 14 bets 4-10 -45.8% [-88.1, +8.0]. By market: spread move-dominated 9-5 (+22%) vs own 2-3; total own-dominated 7-12 vs move-dominated 4-6. None of it is separable from noise (see F6), but the direction is the opposite of "own view is the part that wins".

Lead's P1, rebuilt independently (script 05, all games, not only bets): corr(model - 11 am line, open - 11 am line) = 0.78 spread (n=138), 0.54 total (n=137) on the snapshot lines (lead 0.79 / 0.58); 0.78 / 0.45 on the model's true open and the logged run-time line (n=145). Mechanism: the spread model's own view differs from the open by only sd 1.0-1.1 points while the open-to-11 am move has sd 1.4-1.6, so any spread edge above the 2.66-point threshold is mostly the market's move; for totals own-view sd (2.5) exceeds move sd (1.9). Share of larger-edge games on the fading side: spread 17 of 17 (100%) on the snapshots, 14 of 14 on true open; total 15 of 21 (71%) on snapshots, 15 of 25 (60%) on true open and logged line. So the lead's "85-90%" is confirmed for spreads (and ML bets) and not for totals (60-71%).

Pushes: a push is graded as a loss (-100) in the simulation (app.py:528 and 537 for spread, 552 and 558 for total: `won` stays False) and in Recent Performance (`spread_correct` app.py:398-400, `total_correct` 414-416 are False on a tie); the tab's record tuple hard-codes pushes = 0 (app.py:1074); `betting.cover_probability` (betting.py:107-121) treats whole-number lines as refunds.
